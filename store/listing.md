## Full description

**English**

OMC IPTV is a media player. It does not include any channels, movies or series. You add the account details or playlist link from your own streaming provider, and the app plays what that provider gives you.

- Live channels with the current and next programme
- Movies and series with seasons and episodes
- Add several accounts (Xtream login or M3U playlist) and switch between them
- Favorites, continue watching, and recently watched channels
- Audio and subtitle selection, playback speed, sleep timer
- Parental control with a PIN that hides adult sections
- Arabic and English, dark and light themes, made for remote controls and touch screens

The first 60 days are free. After that the app needs an activation code from the seller.

OMC IPTV does not provide or promote unlicensed content. You are responsible for the source of what you play.
