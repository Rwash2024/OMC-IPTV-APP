Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

OMC IPTV is a video player that shows channels, movies and series from a streaming service the user chooses. The app does not provide or host any content. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

Who is responsible

Mohamed Rwash. Contact: mohamed.adel.rwash@gmail.com or WhatsApp +201022097217.

Data kept on your device only

  • The streaming account details you enter: server address, username and password, or an M3U playlist link.
  • Favorites, where you stopped in movies, recently watched channels, app settings, and the parental-control PIN (stored as a salted hash, not as the number).

This data is never sent to us. It is sent only to the streaming provider you chose, so it can show you content.

Data sent to our server

To run the free trial and activation codes, the app sends us:

  • A device ID: on a phone, a value derived from the device identifier and turned into a hash (not the identifier itself); on Samsung TVs, the TV's MAC address.
  • The system type (Android or TV), the app version, and the dates of first and last use.
  • The activation codes you enter and the result (success or failure).

We need this to know when a trial or subscription starts, and to stop one code being used on several devices.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone data, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools. If you contact us yourself by WhatsApp or email, we see only what you send.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database with device IDs and subscription status (servers in Europe).
  • GitHub hosts the app files, updates and this website.
  • Google Fonts: the app and these web pages load their fonts from Google, which sees your IP address when they load.
  • Your streaming provider: its own policy governs the data you send to it.

How long we keep data

We keep device and activation records while the app is in use. You can ask us to delete your device's data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. To remove the data on your device, delete your account in the app's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not aimed at children. Its parental control hides adult sections behind a PIN, but the content available is chosen by the account holder with their provider.

Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS, and the database tables are locked so only the administrator can read them. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version here with a new date.
